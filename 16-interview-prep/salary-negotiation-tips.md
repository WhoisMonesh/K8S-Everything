## Negotiation Strategies

### 1. Research Market Value

```bash
# Research resources
- Glassdoor
- Levels.fyi
- LinkedIn Salary
- Payscale
- Blind (app)
```

### 2. Highlight Certifications

| Certification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| CKA | Shows cluster administration skills |
| CKAD | Shows application development skills |
| CKS | Shows security expertise |
| AWS/GCP/Azure | Shows cloud platform expertise |

### 3. Quantify Impact

| Metric | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Cost savings | "Reduced infrastructure costs by 30%" |
| Uptime | "Achieved 99.99% uptime" |
| Deployment frequency | "Increased deployment frequency by 5x" |
| Recovery time | "Reduced MTTR from 4 hours to 30 minutes" |
| Team productivity | "Saved team 20 hours/week with automation" |

### 4. Negotiate Beyond Salary

| Benefit | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Stock options | $10K-$100K+ |
| Signing bonus | $5K-$25K |
| Annual bonus | 10-20% of salary |
| Remote work | $5K-$15K value |
| Learning budget | $2K-$5K/year |
| Conference budget | $1K-$3K/year |
| PTO | Negotiable |

## Negotiation Script

### Initial Offer

```
"Thank you for the offer. I'm very excited about the opportunity.
Based on my research and experience, I was expecting a salary in
the range of $[X]-$[Y]. Is there flexibility in the compensation
package?"
```

### Counter Offer

```
"I appreciate the offer. Based on my [X] years of experience with
Kubernetes, my [certifications], and my track record of [quantified
impact], I believe a salary of $[X] would be more appropriate.
I'm also open to discussing [signing bonus/stock options/remote work]."
```

### Final Offer

```
"Thank you for working with me on this. I'm happy to accept the
offer at $[X] with [benefits]. When can I expect the written offer?"
```

## Red Flags to Avoid

| Red Flag | What It Means |
|----------|---------------|
| "We're like a family" | Overwork culture |
| "Fast-paced environment" | Chaos, no processes |
| "Wear many hats" | Understaffed |
| "Competitive salary" | Low pay |
| "Unlimited PTO" | No PTO policy |
| "Equity" (no salary) | Startup risk |

## Questions to Ask

### About the Role

```
- What does a typical day look like?
- What are the biggest challenges facing the team?
- How is success measured in this role?
- What's the team structure?
- What's the on-call rotation?
```

### About Compensation

```
- What's the salary range for this role?
- What's the bonus structure?
- What's the equity/stock option plan?
- What's the benefits package?
- What's the PTO policy?
```

### About Growth

```
- What's the career path for this role?
- What training/certifications do you support?
- What's the promotion timeline?
- What's the team's growth plan?
```
