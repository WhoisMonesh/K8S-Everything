# Companies Using Kubernetes

> **Category:** Reference / Case Studies

## What It Is

A comprehensive list of major companies and organizations that have adopted Kubernetes in production, organized by industry vertical. Each entry includes a link to its detailed case study in [Company Cases](17-company-cases/).

## Why It Exists

Understanding how leading companies use Kubernetes helps you:
- Learn production best practices
- Understand real-world architecture decisions
- Evaluate which companies are hiring K8s experts
- Find interview talking points
